What he does

Six areas of practice

Six connected ways of working with leaders and their organizations.

Executive coaching and personal branding

Be the architect of your own career. Leaders build a personal brand strategy, find their real strengths and drivers, and work through the habits that hold them back.

Global leadership and talent development

Programmes that keep high potential people, build succession plans with diversity in view, and line talent strategy up with where the business is going.

Diversity, inclusion and equity

An open culture strengthens the relationship between employer and employee, and lifts productivity and satisfaction. The work covers attracting and keeping a diverse workforce.

Unconscious bias and micro inequities

An interactive framework that raises awareness and turns it into behaviour people can actually use. It improves relationships and effectiveness across the organization.

Team transformation

Moving teams from conflict to collaboration, from stagnation to innovation, and from inefficiency to effectiveness. Differences become something the team uses.

Sustaining innovation excellence

Working with leadership to change patterns and engineer transformation across processes, products, customer interfaces and the way the organization thinks.

Purpose

“Enabling individuals, teams and organizations to transition from their present levels of effectiveness to higher levels of impact.”
