About Philip Berry

Being better than you believe

Philip is passionate about the pursuit of excellence in the global workplace through human resources optimization. He brings in-depth perspective to cross-cultural talent development, diversity and inclusion, executive coaching and personal branding — perspective developed through decades in senior roles at major corporations.

He has held senior positions at Colgate-Palmolive, the Clinton Foundation, Procter & Gamble, Digital Equipment Corporation and the Triborough Bridge and Tunnel Authority, and has implemented organizational plans in more than sixty countries.

What he does

Six areas of practice

Philip Berry Associates works with leaders and their organizations across six connected areas of practice.

Executive Coaching & Personal Branding

Be the architect of your career in the dynamic and complex global environment. Leaders develop a personal brand strategy that increases their effectiveness, explore their strengths and drivers, and work through the self-limiting behaviours that prevent advancement.

Global Leadership & Talent Development

Programmes that develop and retain high-potential employees, build succession planning with diversity firmly in view, and align talent strategy with future business needs in order to stimulate extraordinary performance.

Diversity, Inclusion & Equity

An open corporate culture that embraces inclusiveness strengthens the employer-employee relationship and increases productivity, effectiveness and employee satisfaction. The work covers attracting and retaining a diverse workforce and uncovering unconscious bias.

Unconscious Bias & Micro-inequities

A framework that increases awareness and facilitates impactful behaviours that create success. The approach is interactive and is designed to improve relationships and effectiveness across the organization.

Team Transformation

Helping teams move from conflict to collaboration, from stagnation to innovation, and from inefficiency to effectiveness. Outcomes include team alignment, stronger working relationships, and differences leveraged for productivity.

Sustaining Innovation Excellence

Working with leadership to change patterns, and to catalyze and engineer transformation across business processes, products and services, customer interfaces, and the way the organization thinks.
